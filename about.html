About Menswear Deal Desk

Menswear Deal Desk is an automated deal scanner with a human curator behind it. A scanner checks retailer sale inventories and deal forums every two hours; every candidate is then graded on brand authenticity, verified discount depth (against the retailer's own list price), and whether the price is genuinely notable.

We only post a deal when the numbers actually deserve it — jewelry at 20% off and "sale" items at retail price get thrown out. If a price claim appears anywhere on this site or our social posts, it was checked against the retailer's live page.

Affiliate disclosure: some outbound links are affiliate links. If you purchase through one, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you. Commissions never influence what gets posted: deals are ranked algorithmically before any monetization decision is made, and we turn down paid placements on weak deals.
